FAQ

Common questions about digital marketing.

What is Local SEO?

Local SEO improves a business's relevance and visibility for location-related searches through suitable website content, accurate business information and other legitimate optimization work.

Can Google Business Profile optimization guarantee top rankings?

No. Google rankings depend on multiple factors, including relevance, distance and prominence. No professional can honestly guarantee a specific Google position.

How long does SEO take to show progress?

Results vary by competition, website condition, market and work completed. Progress should be assessed using Search Console, analytics and relevant business performance data.

What information do you need to get started?
